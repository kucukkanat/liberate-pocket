01 / What you control

The hardware is yours.
Let's keep it that way.

A community-powered toolkit for discovering what your Pocket can do without depending on a proprietary service.

⌁

Direct Bluetooth

Connect straight from supported browsers via Web Bluetooth. No companion desktop daemon required.

◇

Device insights

Explore GATT services and inspect the BLE protocol, characteristics, and device responses.

↓

Recording access

Query and retrieve recordings with the experimental BLE transfer implementation. Test with your firmware.

⌘

Local by design

Your session key stays in memory in your browser. No account registration or analytics endpoint.

↗

Open, inspectable code

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Audit it, fork it, and help make device independence better.

∞

Built to outlast clouds

Experimental foundations for a future where hardware remains usable independently of its vendor.

02 / Getting started

Three steps to freedom.

01 / OPEN

Launch the explorer

Use desktop Chrome over HTTPS. Your browser asks permission to access Bluetooth.

02 / CONNECT

Select your Pocket

Turn on your device and disconnect it from the official app before connecting.

03 / EXPLORE

Discover what it exposes

Inspect GATT characteristics first. Use your existing session key for authenticated commands.

THE NEXT CHAPTER IS OPEN

Freedom looks good on your Pocket.

No sign-up. No servers in the middle. Just you, your device, and a browser. Some protocol features remain experimental and depend on firmware.
